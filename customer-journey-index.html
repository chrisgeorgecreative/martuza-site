Case study 02 · Customer experience

The Customer Journey Index

Mapping planned digital support across common customer experiences.

Role
Service designer, lead
Timeline
2023 — 4 months
Focus
Digital channel strategy
Hero image — 16:9

The challenge

Customer support was planned channel-by-channel, with no shared view of the experiences it was meant to serve.

Teams optimised their own touchpoints while gaps and overlaps between channels went unseen — there was no single place to see how support lined up against the journeys customers actually moved through.

Placeholder — describe the problem, the constraints, and why it mattered.

The approach

I built the Customer Journey Index — one structured map of planned digital support across the most common experiences.

Each experience was broken into stages, then cross-referenced against the channels slated to support it, exposing where coverage was thin, duplicated, or missing.

Placeholder — walk through your process and the key decisions you made.

The outcome

The Index became the shared reference every team planned against.

It turned scattered, channel-by-channel decisions into a coordinated view, surfaced priority gaps for investment, and gave the group one language for journey planning.
