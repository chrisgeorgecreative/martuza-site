I'm a service designer and design leader with deep roots in storytelling and customer experience. My work sits at the intersection of people, process, and possibility — translating complex organizational challenges into clear, human-centered service visions that teams can actually build toward.

My philosophy is simple: before you can design the how, you have to deeply understand the who. That means listening first, mapping what's true, then building bridges between what customers experience and what organizations can deliver.

Currently focused on digital transformation in the energy sector, bringing enterprise-scale service design discipline — blueprinting, journey management, cross-channel experience strategy — to problems that affect millions of customers.

Experience Management · Service Design
Journey & Experience Management
Unifying all CX documentation under one umbrella for rapid indexing and insight generation
+
The Challenge

Customer experience documentation was fragmented across teams, tools, and formats — journey maps in Miro, service blueprints in decks, research insights in spreadsheets. No single source of truth meant that teams were duplicating effort, drawing inconsistent conclusions, and struggling to connect the dots between touchpoints and business outcomes.

The organization needed a way to make its accumulated CX knowledge findable, usable, and alive — not archived and forgotten.

My Role

Led the strategic design and facilitation effort to define what an integrated experience management system would look like — from taxonomy to tooling to team adoption. Facilitated cross-functional working sessions to align stakeholders on a shared documentation framework.

Outcomes & Impact
  • Established a unified CX documentation taxonomy adopted across product, design, and customer ops teams
  • Reduced time-to-insight for new project onboarding by surfacing relevant journey artifacts in a single indexed repository
  • Enabled cross-functional teams to identify experience gaps and overlaps that were previously invisible
  • Created a living system — not a static archive — with governance model to keep documentation current
02
Strategic Design · Digital Experience
The Customer Journey Index
A comprehensive outlook for how all common customer experiences will be supported across digital channels
+
The Challenge

Digital transformation in a large organization means more than launching new channels — it requires clarity about which experiences will be supported, how, and when. Without a deliberate planning framework, digital investments risk solving the wrong problems or creating new inconsistencies.

The organization needed a strategic map of its intended digital experience landscape: a "north star" document that could coordinate roadmap decisions across product, engineering, and customer experience teams.

My Role

Designed and led the creation of the Customer Journey Index — a structured artifact that catalogues all major customer experience scenarios, maps them to digital channel coverage, and identifies gaps, priorities, and interdependencies. Drove cross-functional alignment across multiple business units.

Outcomes & Impact
  • Delivered the organization's first comprehensive view of digital experience coverage across all major customer scenarios
  • Surfaced critical gaps between customer expectations and planned digital capabilities, informing roadmap prioritization
  • Became a shared reference artifact used by product, UX, and strategy teams to align investment decisions
  • Established a repeatable framework for ongoing experience planning as digital capabilities evolve
03
Service Blueprint · End-to-End Design
Meter to Cash Service Blueprint
A comprehensive portrait of the end-to-end front and backstage experience from meter install to first payment
+
The Challenge

The meter-to-cash journey — from initial meter installation through account activation, billing, and first payment receipt — touches dozens of teams, systems, and customer interactions. Despite being foundational to the business, it had never been fully documented as a single coherent experience.

Siloed process maps existed in individual departments, but no one had the full picture. This made it nearly impossible to diagnose failure points, design improvements, or onboard new team members with a clear understanding of the whole.

My Role

Led a comprehensive research and blueprinting effort spanning multiple business functions. Conducted stakeholder interviews and process deep-dives to surface both the customer-facing journey and the internal operations supporting it. Synthesized findings into a layered service blueprint capturing frontstage interactions, backstage processes, and supporting systems.

Outcomes & Impact
  • Produced the organization's first end-to-end service blueprint spanning the full Meter-to-Cash lifecycle
  • Identified previously undocumented handoff failures and friction points across departmental boundaries
  • Became the foundational reference for a multi-year digital transformation initiative targeting the onboarding experience
  • Used as an onboarding tool for new employees and a shared language artifact for cross-functional project teams
